# Ride Height

Ride height is the distance the chassis sits above the ground. Changing ride height of the car is also another powerful way to control the car balance as it can affect the amount of aero downforce generated at both ends of the of the car and also impacts lateral weight transfer by changing the CG of the car.

### Ride Height effect on downforce and drag

Each car in iRacing will have an optimal ride height at which it will generate the most downforce and a different lower ride height at which it will generate the least amount of drag. Often for each car iRacing will include these optimal ride height details in the default iRacing setups under the notes tab.

Of course, your ride height will be very dynamic as you drive the car so keeping it within an optimal window for downforce and drag is a key element to car setup.

For tracks like Daytona or Lemans where top speed is more critical you will want to see how low you can go with the ride height without losing to much of the aero downforce in the corners.

For example, if you go too low on the rear ride height, you might end up losing rear grip in a high speed corner because the rear ride height is too low and outside an optimal aero downforce window reducing the rear downforce and thus rear grip.

### Ride Height effect on lateral weight transfer and thus lateral grip

As discussed in the weight transfer section, as you raise the CG (Center of Gravity) of a car this will increase the lateral weight transfer of the car and cause it to lose lateral grip. So, if you want the car to have more oversteer then you can raise the rear ride height to reduce the lateral grip at the rear. Likewise, if you decrease the rear ride height you will increase the lateral grip at the rear, as long as you don't go too far and put the car out of the optimal aero downforce window.

### Handling Scenarios

In each of the following scenarios, here are things you will help you improve the handling by making changes to front and rear ride height. You don't need to do all of the suggested changes listed below in these scenarios. It is best to start at the top of the list and do one at a time.

#### Loose Off Power

* lower rear ride height
* Increase front ride height

#### Loose On Power

* lower rear ride height
* Increase front ride height

#### Push Off Power

* Increase rear ride height
* Lower front ride height

#### Push On Power

* Increase rear ride height
* Lower front ride height
