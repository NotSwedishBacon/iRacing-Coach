# Anti-Roll Bars (ARB)

Anti-Roll bars or Swaybars are designed to reduce the amount the car leans/rolls which affects how fast the car reacts, and the overall feel. However, swaybars increase the difference in vertical load on the inside and outside tires, which actually reduces overall lateral grip.

So why use swaybars if they take away lateral grip?

* Swaybars reduce chassis roll making the car feel more predictable and easier to drive.
* Swaybars allow you to control the balance of the car by taking away grip from one end of the car.

### How does it work

As illustrated in the diagram below, as the car leans into the corner the swaybar connected to the outside tire's suspension arm, is pushed up causing the inside tire's suspension arm to be pushed up as well, making the car corner flatter yes, but more importantly *cause the outside tire to have more load than the inside tire.* Remember that the greater the difference in load between the outside tire and the inside tire, the less lateral grip those two tires will make. Let's understand this in more detail.

The Lateral grip for a tire increases with weight loading, but is not linear. Increasing load on a tire by 50% may only increase lateral grip by 30%. This represents a reduction of grip efficiency. That is, if you overload one tire by reducing load on the other tire (on the same axle), the total lateral grip is reduced.

Since the stiffer the swaybar, the greater difference in load between the inside and outside tire, then it follows that a stiffer swaybar produces less lateral grip. This is based on the theory behind weight transfer, so if you want to learn more read the [Weight Transfer Section](/learn-car-setup-for-iracing/weight-transfer.md).

### Handling Scenarios

In each of the following scenarios, here are things you will help you improve the handling by making changes to front and rear swaybars (ARB). You don't need to do all of the suggested changes listed below in these scenarios. It is best to start at the top of the list and do one at a time.

#### Loose Off Power

* Stiffer front ARB
* Softer rear ARB

#### Loose On Power

* Stiffer front ARB
* Softer rear ARB

#### Push Off Power

* Softer front ARB
* Stiffer rear ARB

#### Push On Power

* Softer front ARB
* Stiffer rear ARB
