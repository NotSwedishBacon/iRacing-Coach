# Team Endurance Race Planner

I created a rather robust google sheet that allows you to plan your stints for team endurance races.  It factors in pit stop times, and even allows you to account for drivers having different pace. 

You can access the sheet [here ](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Xm9K98l7GUxSIObEaLNvxV2FQB_2xNZ_omNA97hFCls/edit?usp=sharing)and then make a copy of it if you wish to edit and use the sheet.
